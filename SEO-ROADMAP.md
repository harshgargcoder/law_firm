This roadmap is tailored to the current Next.js website. SEO improves visibility over time; completing the checklist does not guarantee immediate rankings.

- [x] Deploy the Next.js website on HTTPS

- [ ] Configure `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` in Vercel (the current Vercel URL is the code fallback)
- [ ] Verify the domain in Google Search Console

## Recommended order

### Phase 1 — Make the website real and indexable

1. Buy the firm’s domain and enable HTTPS.
2. Deploy the Next.js website.
3. Set `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` to the exact public URL, for example:

   ```env
   NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL=https://www.your-real-domain.com
   ```

4. Confirm these URLs work:

   - `/robots.txt`
   - `/sitemap.xml`
   - Every main navigation page

### Phase 2 — Improve page relevance

Create useful, original content for the people who may hire the firm. Prioritize:

- Constitutional law
- Civil litigation
- Commercial disputes
- Supreme Court of India representation
- Delhi High Court representation
- Legal internship program
- Contact/location page for New Delhi

Each important page should have one clear topic, one descriptive H1, useful supporting sections, internal links, and a clear contact action.

### Phase 3 — Local SEO and trust

1. Create or claim Google Business Profile if the office is eligible to receive clients at the listed address.
2. Keep the firm name, address, phone number, email, hours, and website identical everywhere.
3. Add the firm to legitimate legal directories, bar/community listings, and professional profiles.
4. Collect genuine client or professional reviews where legally and ethically appropriate.

### Phase 4 — Google Search Console

1. Verify ownership of the domain.
2. Submit `https://www.your-real-domain.com/sitemap.xml`.
3. Inspect the homepage and important service pages.
4. Monitor indexing, search queries, page experience, and errors monthly.

### Phase 5 — Authority and maintenance

- Publish genuinely useful legal updates instead of thin or copied articles.
- Earn relevant backlinks through legal publications, professional associations, events, and citations.
- Review titles, broken links, contact details, and indexing issues monthly.
- Revalidate structured data after major content or address changes.

## Important notes for this website

- The technical SEO foundation is already implemented in the code.
- The current `.example` email address must be replaced before launch.
- The public domain must be configured before submitting the sitemap.
- Do not create artificial, paid, spammy, or unrelated backlinks.
- Avoid making unsupported claims such as guaranteed results or “best lawyer” language.
